Hermes

Hermes is my personal AI assistant. It runs on my own computer, and I'm the only one who uses it.

What it does with Google

Hermes signs in to my own Google account so it can help me with:

  • Gmail - reading, sorting and sending my email
  • Calendar - checking and adding events
  • Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides - finding, reading and editing my files
  • Contacts - looking people up (read only)
  • Tasks - reading and adding to my to-do lists
  • YouTube - managing my own playlists
  • Gmail alerts - knowing when new mail comes in
