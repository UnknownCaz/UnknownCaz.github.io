Hermes privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Hermes is my personal AI assistant, and I'm its only user. This page explains what it does with data from my Google account.

What it can access

Only my own Google account: Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Contacts, Tasks and YouTube.

How it uses that data

Only to do what I ask, like reading an email, adding a calendar event or finding a file. It never uses my data for ads, and it never sells it.

Where it goes

Hermes keeps its data on my own computers. To understand what I'm asking, it sends the text it's working on to the AI model it runs on (right now, Anthropic's Claude API). Nothing is shared with anyone else.

Taking access away

I can remove Hermes's access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
